# AI authorship and responsibility

The initiative was planned, implemented, analyzed, and packaged by Codex AI
agents under human-specified scope, compute, privacy, and publication
constraints. A separate agent reviewed the protocol and conditional control
before outcomes; the terminal result received same-owner inspection because
nested task creation was unavailable. Neither form is human peer review.

The package is a local research artifact. No remote repository, paper,
publication, or external claim has been authorized. A human adopter remains
responsible for checking licenses, rerunning the code, reviewing the evidence,
and deciding whether any result should be shared.
